# FuelPass — Project Overview

> _Powering Aviation_

## What FuelPass Is

FuelPass is a **neutral, digital marketplace for aviation fuel **. It connects airlines and fuel resellers directly on a single platform — replacing email/phone/PDF/spreadsheet workflows with efficient, transparent, and reliable digital procurement.

## Who We Serve

| Stakeholder | Needs |
| --- | --- |
| **Airlines** | Lower fuel costs, transparency, real-time offer comparison, operational reliability |
| **Reseller** | Predictable demand, reduced documentation burden, forecasting, transparent pricing |

## Product Principles

- **Single source of truth** — supplier pricing and offer data structured into consistent, trusted records.
- **Transparency** — prices, offers, and statuses are instantly comparable and unambiguous.
- **Neutrality** — a marketplace, not a reseller; the platform never favors one party.
- **Reliability** — high performance, security, and uptime; numbers users can trust.
- **Focused scope** — centered on fuel procurement: RFQs, pricing, offers, and orders.

## Domain Glossary

- **RFQ (Request for Quote)** — a buyer's request for fuel pricing at a given location/airport.
- **Offer** — a supplier's quote in response to an RFQ.
- **Order** — a booking for fuel at an airport, for a specific aircraft, fuel type, and volume.
- **Fuel types** — e.g. Jet A-1 (turbine) and Avgas (piston aircraft).
- **Airport** — identified by its ICAO code (e.g. EDDF, EGLL, KJFK).
- **Aircraft** — identified by its tail/registration number.
